# Structural organization

Criterion handbook. Assessment procedure: see `instruction.md`.

The sample corrections below show which function fits which section. In the fix hint in `problem` for the article under review, take the task, procedure, and data only from that article.

## Definition

**Structural organization** is the distribution of the article's content by function: the introduction prepares the task, the task is stated explicitly, the main body separates others' approaches from the author's own material and method, the results section presents the outcome, the discussion interprets it, and the conclusions close what was stated and introduce no new data. Matching headings to a single template does not by itself satisfy the criterion.

A violation arises when a required function is not performed anywhere or is performed somewhere other than where the reader can check its basis.

## Assessment questions

| Feature | Question | Violation if |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introduction | Does it prepare the task and hold no results or conclusions? | no |
| Task | Is there a question, aim, or hypothesis against which the outcome can be checked? | no |
| Others' and own | Are the sources separated from the author's material, method, and observations? | no |
| Order | Are the material and the analysis rule presented in a section before the interpretation? | no |
| Results | Does the results section hold the outcome rather than review or method material? | no |
| Discussion | Is there a comparison with the task, with other works, and with limitations? | no |
| Conclusions | Do they close the questions posed, and do they introduce no new material? | no |
| Headings | Is the section's function performed regardless of how the section is titled? | no |

## Violation types

### 1. No testable task

Features: task, introduction.

Error: there is no question, aim, or hypothesis from which one can understand why the material was collected and which result counts as an answer.

Off-function:

> The introduction says that feedback is important and that digital tools are developing. Questionnaires and tables are then described immediately. There is no question, aim, or hypothesis in the text.

Correction:

> After the review of the problem, the task is stated: to establish whether the share of uncorrected factual errors differs between a step-by-step checklist and a final comment only. The method and results that follow are subordinated to this comparison.

Rule: the task must make it possible to check the method, results, and conclusions against it. If the article has neither a question nor material from which a question already follows, do not invent a task. In the problem, write that there is no testable task.

### 2. Ready-made result in the introduction

Features: introduction.

Error: the introduction reports results or a conclusion that belong in the results and conclusions.

Off-function:

> Already in the third paragraph of the introduction it says: "With the checklist, the share of factual errors fell from 18% to 11%."

Correction:

> At the end of the introduction it says: "Below, the checklist is compared with the final comment by the share of uncorrected factual errors." The values themselves are given in the results, and the conclusion in the conclusions.

Rule: in the introduction, keep the announcement of what is being compared. In the problem, move the result and the conclusion to the results and conclusions. The fix hint for the introduction brings in no values that should not be in the introduction.

### 3. Method after interpretation

Features: order.

Error: the interpretation and the numbers stand in a section that comes before the section giving the rule by which they can be trusted.

Off-function:

> The author first explains why the checklist is more effective, then gives figures, and only in the last section reports who was in the groups and how errors were counted.

Correction:

> The composition of the groups, the type of assignment, and the error-counting rule are presented before the results. The discussion begins after these rules and the obtained values are already known to the reader.

Rule: place the section with the basis for interpretation before the section with the interpretation. Do not add new counting rules. If the rules are in the article but come late, indicate the relocation in the problem, and do not suggest changing their content.

### 4. Others' and own work not separated

Features: others' and own.

Error: another study's result and the author's own observation stand as one continuous chain, although they have different grounds.

Off-function:

> The results section says: "Corrections become more frequent with step-by-step commenting. In our group, 64% of comments were corrected." The first sentence repeats the finding of Lebedev (2021), cited in the review, without marking it as his.

Correction:

> The review separately reports: "Lebedev (2021) reports more frequent corrections with step-by-step commenting." The results report only the author's own value: 64% of comments corrected.

Rule: separate the other study's result and the author's own observation. Take the year and wording from the article.

### 5. Results section holds material of other sections

Features: results.

Error: the results section is occupied by review material or a description of the method instead of the result.

Off-function:

> The results section opens with two pages on the history of checklists and then describes how the questionnaires were handed out.

Correction:

> The history of checklists is moved to the review, and the questionnaire procedure to the method. The results section begins with the comparison of the two checking methods by the share of factual errors.

Rule: in the results, keep the result. Return review material to the review and the procedure to the method.

### 6. Discussion without comparison

Features: discussion.

Error: the discussion does not relate the result to the task, to other works, and to the limitations.

Off-function:

> The discussion says that checklists are promising and that digital methods are developing. It does not relate the difference between the groups to the task, to the work of Lebedev (2021), or to the limit of a single seminar.

Correction:

> The discussion briefly recalls the main difference, then compares it with the task of comparing two checking methods, notes that its direction is similar to Lebedev's work (2021), and limits the conclusion to one seminar and one type of error.

Rule: the discussion performs the comparison. Do not add a work or a limit that the article does not name. If there is no comparison, write in the problem which of the three links is missing: with the task, with other works, or with a limitation.

### 7. Conclusions introduce new material

Features: conclusions.

Error: the conclusions report for the first time data or a procedure that the reader has not seen before.

Off-function:

> The conclusion introduces, for the first time, data on a repeat check six months later.

Correction:

> The delayed-check data are moved to the method and the results. The conclusions retain only the consequence that these sections already contain. If there was no such check, the item is removed from the conclusions.

Rule: return new material to the sections where it can be substantiated. If the article has no such procedure, the item is removed from the conclusions rather than added retroactively.

### 8. Conclusion item does not close the task

Features: conclusions, task.

Error: one of the questions posed has disappeared. A topic that answers none of the questions posed has been added to the conclusions.

Off-function:

> The task included two questions: the number of errors and the checking time. The conclusions report only the reduction in errors and then move on to the general benefits of digitalization.

Correction:

> The first item of the conclusions concerns the share of errors, the second concerns the checking time. If time was not measured, the conclusions state explicitly that the second research question remained unanswered. The general topic of digitalization is not added to the conclusions.

Rule: the composition of the conclusion repeats the composition of the task. Call an unclosed question unclosed. Do not add a topic outside the task to the conclusions. Do not invent a result that the article does not contain.

## Acceptable composition

A journal may name sections differently. Do not add such a fragment to the list if the function is performed where its basis can be checked.

Functions displaced:

> In the "Introduction" the final result is already given, in the "Results" a new literature review is given, and in the "Conclusions" data appear that were not there before.

Functions preserved under different headings:

> The task is stated at the end of the review, the procedure in the section "Material and Course of Analysis", and the outcome in the "Conclusion", which answers only this task.

## Markers

Check these words first. A word by itself is not a violation: the questions and the type decide.

- Results in the introduction: the study proved, we found, the share fell — in the introduction.
- Others' and own glued together: according to the author's data, in our group — in one paragraph without a boundary.
- Empty discussion: a discussion that does not refer to the task, other works, or a limit.
- New material in the conclusions: for the first time, moreover, six months later — if the procedure did not appear earlier.
- Usually keep: below ... is compared, the task is to, additional observation, remains unanswered, compared.
